# Repealed rules

Rules removed from the ruleset, with their numbers and their last text. Their numbers are never reused.

**35.** While the backup Operator acts as Operator, the absent Operator's agreement is not required for a proposal to be adopted.

*Repealed by [Proposal #9](https://github.com/archetech/archonomicon/pull/9): once no player's agreement is required, it had no effect.*

**30.** No rule may claim precedence over rule 29.

*Repealed by [Proposal #10](https://github.com/archetech/archonomicon/pull/10): once rule 29 is immutable, a mutable rule's claim of precedence over it already loses.*
